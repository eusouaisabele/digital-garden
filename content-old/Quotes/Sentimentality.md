**PT**

minha mãe achava estudo a coisa mais fina do mundo. não é. a coisa mais fina do mundo é o sentimento.

naquele dia, fazia um azul tão límpido, meu deus, que eu me sentia perdoado para sempre. nem sei de quê.

é horrível assistir à agonia de uma esperança.

**EN**

my heart was a hysterical unreliable organ.

i am someone who thinks and feels much more than is reasonable - and that is all.

i remember awakening one morning and finding everything smeared with the color of forgotten love.

i am eternally, devastatingly romantic, and i thought people would see it because "romantic" doesn't mean "sugary". it's dark and tormented- the furor of passion, the despair of an idealism that you can't attain.

i can barely conceive a type of beauty in which there is no melancholy.

everything is strange. things are huge and very small.

you care and it’s all over the place.

i read a lot. i listen a lot. i think a lot. but so little remains. the books i read, their plots, their protagonists fade… names of people, books, cities. they are already fading away. even the titles of films i've seen recently… they have already faded… everything that i see, or read, or listen to, connects, translates into moods, bits of surroundings, colours… with me, everything is mood, mood, or else… simply nothingness.

you don't argue with feelings, but beliefs you can challenge.
