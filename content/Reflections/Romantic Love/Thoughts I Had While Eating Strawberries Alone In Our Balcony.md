I always had this  
huge impression  
that something extraordinary  
was about to happen.

I still think about it sometimes.

Just the other day,  
I was eating strawberries on the balcony  
(I came back just in time for the first few days of summer),  
and I found myself  
under that awareness again.

Then my gut also told me —  
when I haven’t even found the words to define  
where this begins or ends —  
that this moment will  
shortly escape me.

The sun sets  
(it didn’t make me warm to begin with).  
There are  
sunnier places and times.
