My arms hold everything within my chest,  
before it all leaks through.  
What I’m made of slowly ceases to exist —  
a very short period,  
a slow and poetical meltdown.  
  
The squared emptiness swallows me again.  
A lot is blank.  
I want it to be about you  
(…) convinced it’s about me.

Then there’s my home, bigger than an ocean:  
beautiful,  
aggressive,  
ephemeral.

There’s me —  
not owning many things  
(exactly three boxes) —  
and it makes me cry.

But I  
am an extension of everything that happened,  
while my footsteps gradually blur.  
And it made me heavy.

I don’t owe you anything.  
Again,  
you see me doing everything.

I reintegrate in methodical speed.  
The radio was on all along.
