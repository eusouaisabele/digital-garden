i used to believe  
the people we meet  
are able to shape us  
a little —  
with their mid-20s experience,  
or  
that crazy ex-relationship,  
or  
something special they’ve  
cooked for you.

now i think  
i was extremely right,  
and that  
we seek and lean  
towards those  
we want to be shaped by.

_(never let anyone define you.)_
