when i got home  
after months  
that felt like years,  
and years  
that felt like seconds —

after i finished  
all that i had to do,  
from meeting relatives  
to giving away the swiss chocolates,  
and showing off  
my bad french,

i went looking for you  
in the places we used  
to exist —

because it’d sound cooler  
if we met spontaneously.

when i didn’t find  
what i looked for (you),  
i started suspecting  
we were no longer real,  
or that you might  
have done the same search  
on a different day.
